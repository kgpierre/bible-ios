PERFORMANCE INTERPRETATION
The 30 timing records and samples in evidence.json are Debug simulator
observations from PerformanceAuditTests, not physical-device guarantees.
Single samples are explicitly identified. The unchanged-configurations metric
measures a batch of 100 calls. Do not compare these values to a device frame
budget without device profiling. Later launch timings were excluded because
other work ran concurrently.
